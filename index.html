How to enquire

  1. Outline your requirement. Note your business location, what you want to finance and the approximate amount.
  2. Speak with Shreekantt. Explain your timeline and share whether you already have a quote or project estimate.
  3. Confirm the next step. Ask which documents may be needed and how to submit them through the appropriate channel.

Common questions

Can I enquire before getting a supplier quote?

Yes. Start with the type of equipment and an estimated budget. A supplier quote can help clarify the price and specifications later.

How much funding can I request?

Start with the amount your business or project needs. Any funding offer depends on the lender, the product and the assessment of your application.

What should I have ready for the first call?

Have the business location, loan purpose, estimated amount and timeline handy. For equipment, bring the supplier estimate if available.
