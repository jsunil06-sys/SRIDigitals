Business Finance

MSME Loans

Discuss funding for operating needs, expansion and growth of a micro, small or medium enterprise.

How MSME finance may help

Use finance to support a clearly identified business need while keeping the repayment structure appropriate for the expected cash flow.

  • Working capital for raw materials, inventory or operational costs
  • Expansion of an existing manufacturing or service business
  • Purchase of tools, machinery or commercial equipment
  • Business premises renovation or additional capacity
  • Vendor, distributor or contractual payment needs where approved

Prepare before enquiring

  • Business activity and age
  • Monthly or annual turnover
  • Funding purpose
  • Required amount and timeline
  • Existing loans or credit facilities

Typical information required

Document needs vary by product, lender and applicant profile.

  • Business and proprietor or partner identification
  • Registration, GST or relevant statutory records
  • Recent bank statements and income or turnover records
  • Proof of business address and operation
  • Estimated cost or invoice for the proposed use of funds

Enquiry process

  1. Define the need. Identify the purpose and amount required.
  2. Share the business profile. Provide a clear description of operations and cash flow.
  3. Review options. A lender or provider assesses eligible products and terms.
  4. Complete verification. Submit required documents and confirm the approved structure.
