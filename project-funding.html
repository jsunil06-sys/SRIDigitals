Project Finance

Project Funding

Organise a funding discussion around your project scope, estimated cost and implementation schedule.

Projects that may be discussed

Project funding enquiries should begin with a practical implementation plan and a clear breakdown of the funding requirement.

  • Industrial plant construction or expansion
  • Infrastructure and commercial development projects
  • Manufacturing capacity and technology projects
  • Contract or order-based project execution
  • Equipment, installation and project mobilisation costs

Prepare a project summary

  • Project objective
  • Location and timeline
  • Total estimated cost
  • Funding amount requested
  • Promoter contribution
  • Contracts or orders

Typical information required

Larger or complex projects require additional verification and may involve several stages of assessment.

  • Detailed project report or business plan
  • Cost estimates, quotations and implementation schedule
  • Promoter, ownership and corporate records
  • Cash-flow projections and assumed revenue basis
  • Permits, approvals or contracts relevant to the project

Enquiry process

  1. Submit the concept. Share the project scope and expected funding timeline.
  2. Assess feasibility. Information is reviewed for completeness and likely financing routes.
  3. Structure the requirement. A lender or provider may request a detailed project report.
  4. Complete due diligence. Final approval depends on credit, project and document review.
