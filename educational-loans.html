Education Finance

Educational Loans

Plan funding for tuition and approved study expenses in India or overseas.

Education expenses that may be covered

Allowances and eligible costs differ among lenders, institutions and study destinations.

  • Tuition and academic fees
  • Hostel or accommodation costs
  • Books and study equipment
  • Travel for overseas study where eligible
  • Living allowance and insurance where approved

Prepare before enquiring

  • Course and institution
  • Admission or offer status
  • Duration and location
  • Total estimated cost
  • Scholarships or sponsor support

Typical information required

Requirements may differ for domestic and international courses.

  • Student identity and address proof
  • Admission and fee documents
  • Co-applicant income and identity records
  • Academic records
  • Collateral documents if required

Enquiry process

  1. Estimate the cost. Include tuition and living expenses.
  2. Share applicant details. Include the student and co-applicant profile.
  3. Review available options. Compare tenure, margin and security requirements.
  4. Submit for assessment. Provide the required academic and financial documents.
