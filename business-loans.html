Business Loans

Explore funding for operations, working capital and planned business growth.

How a business loan may help

Use the loan for a clear business purpose and match the repayment structure to the expected cash flow.

  • Working capital and inventory
  • Business expansion or renovation
  • Marketing and operational expenses
  • Supplier or contract payments
  • Commercial equipment or technology

Prepare before enquiring

  • Business type and age
  • Turnover and profit records
  • Funding purpose
  • Required amount
  • Existing borrowings

Typical information required

Document requirements depend on the lender and applicant.

  • Business registration and identity documents
  • Bank statements
  • GST or statutory records if applicable
  • Income or financial statements
  • Business address proof

Enquiry process

  1. Define the need. Identify purpose and amount.
  2. Share the business profile. Explain operations and cash flow.
  3. Review eligible options. Compare available terms.
  4. Complete assessment. Submit documents and proceed if approved.
