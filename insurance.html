Insurance needs we can discuss

Cover options, exclusions and premiums vary by insurer, policy and applicant profile.

  • Life and term insurance

  • Motor and vehicle insurance

Prepare before enquiring

  • Insurance type
  • Cover required
  • Age or asset details
  • Existing policies
  • Budget or renewal date

Information commonly required

Requirements depend on the policy category.

  • Identity and address proof
  • Medical or lifestyle information where applicable
  • Vehicle or asset information
  • Property or business details
  • Previous policy and claim history

Enquiry process

  1. Identify the risk. Describe what needs protection.
  2. Review cover options. Compare benefit, exclusions and premium.
  3. Disclose relevant details. Provide accurate policy information.
  4. Complete underwriting. Confirm the policy terms and payment.
