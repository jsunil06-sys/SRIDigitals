International Enquiries

NRI Loans

Discuss eligible Indian financing requirements from outside the country, subject to applicable lending and FEMA requirements.

Possible enquiry purposes

Available structures depend on the applicant's residential status, income source, the proposed use of funds and the lender's NRI policy.

  • Business or MSME finance in India
  • Industrial or equipment investment
  • Approved project or commercial funding requirements
  • Business expansion where the applicant meets eligibility rules
  • Property or asset-related requirements only if offered by the lender

Prepare before enquiring

  • Country of residence
  • Residential status
  • Indian and overseas income details
  • Purpose and amount
  • Indian account and address details

Typical information required

Original documents, translations or attestation may be required depending on the lender.

  • Passport, visa or residency information
  • PAN and relevant Indian identification records
  • Proof of overseas and Indian address
  • Income evidence and recent bank statements
  • Business, project or asset documents relevant to the application

Enquiry process

  1. Confirm status and purpose. Explain where you live and what you intend to finance.
  2. Check eligibility. A lender reviews nationality, residency, income and repayment arrangements.
  3. Arrange verification. Identity, overseas income and Indian documents may be validated.
  4. Confirm terms. Approved applications proceed only after applicable terms and legal requirements are finalised.
