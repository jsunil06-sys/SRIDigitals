Personal Loans

Discuss funding for planned personal expenses and short-term financial priorities.

Possible uses

Personal loan requirements depend on income, repayment capacity and lender eligibility.

  • Medical or family expenses
  • Wedding or household expenditure
  • Travel and planned purchases
  • Education or professional development costs
  • Debt consolidation where permitted

Prepare before enquiring

  • Loan purpose
  • Required amount
  • Monthly income
  • Existing obligations
  • Preferred repayment period

Typical information required

Requirements vary by lender and applicant profile.

  • Identity and address proof
  • Income documents or salary records
  • Recent bank statements
  • Employment or business information
  • Details of current loans

Enquiry process

  1. Share the requirement. Explain the purpose and amount.
  2. Review eligibility. Income and obligations are assessed.
  3. Compare options. Review rate, tenure and fees.
  4. Complete verification. Submit documents and finalise approval.
