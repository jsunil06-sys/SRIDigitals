Plan your vehicle finance

The final loan amount and terms depend on the dealer quotation, applicant profile and lender policy.

Prepare before enquiring

  • Vehicle model and variant
  • On-road price
  • Planned down payment
  • Monthly income
  • Purchase timeline

Typical information required

Requirements vary by lender and vehicle dealer.

  • Identity and address proof
  • Income proof and bank statements
  • Vehicle quotation or proforma invoice
  • Down-payment information
  • Existing loan or credit details

Enquiry process

  1. Select the vehicle. Identify the model and on-road price.
  2. Confirm the budget. Decide the amount and repayment preference.
  3. Review offers. Compare eligible terms and charges.
  4. Complete verification. Submit documents for lender assessment.
