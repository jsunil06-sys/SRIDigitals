Industrial Finance

Industrial Loans

Funding discussions for manufacturing units, factory expansion and operational improvements.

What this service can cover

Industrial loan enquiries may cover a new manufacturing facility, expansion of an existing unit, technology upgrades or replacement of critical plant and machinery.

  • Factory expansion, renovation or infrastructure improvement
  • New machinery and production-line installation
  • Modernisation, automation and process improvement
  • Working capital linked to expansion or production needs
  • Supplier payments and project-related expenses where approved

Prepare before enquiring

  • Business location and industry
  • Purpose and project scope
  • Estimated funding amount
  • Expected timeline
  • Supplier or contractor quote, if available

Typical information required

Requirements vary by lender and may change after initial assessment.

  • Business registration and identification documents
  • Business bank statements and financial records
  • Proof of address and ownership or occupancy details
  • Project report, cost estimate and supplier quotations
  • Existing liabilities and facility statements

How the process works

  1. Share the requirement. Explain the purpose, location, project cost and timeline.
  2. Initial discussion. Relevant financing options and document requirements are discussed.
  3. Application review. The lender or authorised provider assesses eligibility and terms.
  4. Sanction and utilisation. If approved, final terms, conditions and disbursement steps are confirmed.
